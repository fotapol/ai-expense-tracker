# Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 18, 2026

AI Expense Tracker is built to help you scan receipts, review spending, and stay organized. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

- Account information such as your name, email address, and authentication details.
- Receipt images and the structured data extracted from those receipts.
- Expense tracking information including categories, labels, budgets, and bill reminders.
- Subscription and billing information needed to manage paid plans.
- Device and usage information that helps us keep the app reliable and secure.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- provide, maintain, and improve AI Expense Tracker;
- process receipt uploads and organize expense records;
- generate insights, analytics, and budget-related features;
- support subscriptions, billing, and account access;
- detect abuse, fraud, and security incidents.

## 3. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect your information. These measures may include encrypted connections, access controls, secure storage practices, backups, and regular operational reviews.

No service can guarantee absolute security, but we work to reduce risk and limit access to personal data to what is necessary to operate and support the product.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information only in limited situations such as:

- with service providers that help us operate the app and infrastructure;
- when required to comply with legal obligations;
- to protect the safety, rights, or security of users or the service;
- as part of a merger, acquisition, or other business transfer.

## 5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

- access the personal information we hold about you;
- request correction of inaccurate data;
- request deletion of your account or stored data;
- object to or limit certain processing;
- withdraw consent where processing depends on consent.

## 6. Cookies And Tracking

The public website may use basic browser storage or similar technologies to remember interface preferences such as theme selection. We may also use limited operational analytics or logging needed to keep the site and app functioning reliably.

## 7. Children's Privacy

AI Expense Tracker is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review and remove it when appropriate.

## 8. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are material, we will update the effective date and may provide additional notice through the app, the website, or email where appropriate.
