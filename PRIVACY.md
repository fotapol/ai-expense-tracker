## 1\. What the App Does

AI Expense Tracker helps users track expenses, upload receipts, scan receipt images, extract receipt data using AI-assisted processing, categorize expenses, view summaries, and access optional premium features through subscriptions.

The App may process receipts and expense records that can include personal, financial, purchase, and transaction-related information.
